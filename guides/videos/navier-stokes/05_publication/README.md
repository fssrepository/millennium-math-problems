# LinkedIn publication package

This directory contains the public English article, its share post, and the
final LinkedIn cover image.

## Files

- `LINKEDIN_AI_VIDEO_EDITING_EN.md` — paste-ready article body.
- `POST_EN.md` — short text for sharing the published article.
- `ai-directed-video-production-linkedin-cover-1920x1080.png` — final 16:9
  article cover with the article title.
- `../renders/edit/navier-stokes-existence-and-smoothness-final-720p.mp4` —
  final 29.7-second vertical video to insert into the article.

## Recommended LinkedIn order

1. On LinkedIn desktop, choose **Write article**.
2. Upload `ai-directed-video-production-linkedin-cover-1920x1080.png` above
   the title as the article cover.
3. Paste the content of `LINKEDIN_AI_VIDEO_EDITING_EN.md`.
4. After the opening paragraph, use **Media** to insert the final MP4.
5. Publish the article, then share it using `POST_EN.md`.

The final video is H.264/AAC, 720×1280, 30 fps and 29.7 seconds, within
LinkedIn's current native-video limits. LinkedIn's separate immersive video
feed cannot be targeted directly; eligible native video posts are selected
for it by LinkedIn.
