standard prompt box. The standard, preferred `Video → Frames` route remains in
`READY_TO_PASTE.md`.

itself spend Flow media credits, but an approved media generation does. Reject
any confirmation that proposes more than one output, a still image, a model
other than Veo 3.1 Lite, or an unexpected credit charge.
